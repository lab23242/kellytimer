# Installing kellytimer

kellytimer is a small timer for focused work. A Chinese landscape painting slowly scrolls behind the time while you work. It takes about a minute to install.

## 1. Download it

Open the download page: **https://github.com/lab23242/kellytimer/releases/latest**

Under **Assets**, click the file whose name ends in **`-setup.exe`**, for example `kellytimer_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe`. It's a small file and downloads in a few seconds.

## 2. Open it

Find the file in your **Downloads** folder and double-click it.

### If Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

This is normal for this app. Click **More info**, then click **Run anyway**.

Windows shows this message for apps from small makers who haven't paid for a security certificate. It's not a sign that anything is wrong.

## 3. Install

Click **Install**. You don't need an administrator password.

On the last screen, leave **Create desktop shortcut** ticked if you'd like an icon on your desktop (you'll find kellytimer in the Start menu either way). Then click **Finish**.

## 4. Using it

The timer appears in the **bottom-right corner** of your screen, just above the clock.

- **Start or pause:** click the time.
- **Move it:** click anywhere on the painting and drag.
- **More buttons:** move your mouse over the timer. Play/pause, skip, start again, settings, and two buttons to tuck it away appear at the top.
- **Smaller view:** double-click the timer to show only the time. Double-click again to go back.
- **Settings:** the little sun-shaped button. Change the lengths, the chime, the size and more. Everything saves by itself.

Each finished focus session leaves a small red seal next to the time. A new painting starts every day.

## Finding it again

When you close kellytimer with the **×**, it keeps running quietly so your timer isn't lost. To bring it back:

1. Look at the bottom-right of your screen, next to the clock.
2. Click the small **^** arrow.
3. Click the red kellytimer icon.

To close it completely, right-click that icon and choose **Quit kellytimer**.

## Uninstalling

Open **Settings** (the Windows one, from the Start menu) → **Apps** → **Installed apps**. Find **kellytimer**, click the **⋯** next to it, then **Uninstall**.
